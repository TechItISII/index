Tech It ISII Unveils New Cyber-Lupa Rebrand

TECH IT ISII INSIDER By Tech It ISII

For the 2026–27 BIOBUZZ™ season, Team #25074 introduces a complete visual rebrand designed to unite our local roots with a modern, high-impact esports aesthetic.

The new crest reimagines the Lupa — historic symbol of Piacenza — fused with cybernetic circuit traces and glowing red optical sensors, framed in Piacenza’s iconic White & Red colors alongside the Italian tricolore.

Representing ISII "Guglielmo Marconi", this new identity marks a sharper, more professional chapter for our engineering, outreach, and competition presence.

// 01 — IDENTITY & HERITAGE

Engineering
Meets Piacenza

We are Tech It ISII (#25074), the official robotics team of IIS "Guglielmo Marconi" (ISII Marconi) in Piacenza, Italy. Bringing together students from Mechanics, Electronics, Computer Science, and Design, we compete in the international FIRST Tech Challenge.

Our new visual identity fuses the pride of our city — Piacenza’s historic White & Red colors and the Lupa emblem — with a high-precision cyber-esports aesthetic. Our goal isn’t just to win on the field, but to build real STEM mastery and inspire the next generation of engineers.

// 04 — SEASON HISTORY

Our
Path

2023–24

CENTERSTAGE™

First participation in the regional competition. We developed a robot capable of placing pixels autonomously.

🌍 Entered the competition
2024–25

INTO THE DEEP™

Complete refactoring of the software architecture and introduction of 4WD Mecanum drivetrain.

💻 Developed foundation
2025–26

DECODE™

A season focused on general enhancing: redesigned chassis, articulated launcher, vision processing, and overall scoring performance.

⚙️ General Enhancing
2026–27

BIOBUZZ™

Part of the FIRST® CANOPY™ season. A biodiversity and pollinator-inspired challenge featuring Pollen scoring elements, precision intaking, and autonomous launching.
